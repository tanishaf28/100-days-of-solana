# Day 7: Sharing My Work and Engaging the #100DaysOfSolana Community

## The Challenge

Today has two parts: share my own work, and engage with other participants' work. Both matter.

### Part 1: Share My Highlight

Post something specific I built, tried, or discovered while working with Solana wallets this week. This can go on social media or on DEV Community, wherever I'm most comfortable.

**What I needed:**

- An account on X (Twitter), LinkedIn, or DEV Community.
- A screenshot, code snippet, or terminal output from one of my wallet experiments.
- Two to three sentences describing what I did and what surprised me.

## Steps

1. Pick a highlight. Look back through the work from the past several days. Which moment stood out? Some ideas:
   - The first time I generated a keypair and saw my public key appear in the terminal.
   - Setting up a wallet like Phantom, Solflare, or Backpack and comparing the experience to something in Web2.
   - Connecting a browser wallet to my web app and seeing my devnet balance appear.
   - A concept that clicked, like how a wallet is really just a keypair, not a "container" for your tokens.
   - Something from my Day 6 write-up about identity on-chain.
2. Capture the evidence. Take a screenshot of the terminal output, the wallet interface, a transaction on the explorer, or a code snippet. Visual proof of the work makes the post more engaging and more useful to other learners.
3. Write the post. Keep it concise. A good formula:
   - What I did: one sentence describing the experiment ("I generated my first Solana keypair using the CLI and sent SOL on devnet.")
   - What surprised me: one sentence about something unexpected or interesting ("The biggest shift from Web2: my identity is just a cryptographic keypair. No username, no email, no password.")
   - What's next: one sentence about what I am looking forward to in the challenge.
4. Tag and publish. Include the hashtag #100DaysOfSolana so other participants and the community can find the post. Tag @solana_devs on X to connect with the broader Solana developer community.

### Part 2: Engage with Other Participants

Amplify days aren't just about broadcasting, they're about building connections. The other people doing this challenge are my peers. Their posts are how I find them, and my engagement is how they find me.

1. Find other posts. Search for #100DaysOfSolana on social media or on DEV Community. I should see posts from other participants who are on the same journey.
2. Read, react, and comment. Pick at least two posts from other participants. Leave a meaningful comment, not just "nice post," but something specific. Maybe I learned something from their take, or hit the same gotcha they mentioned, or have a question about their approach. A good comment starts a conversation.
3. Share someone else's work. If a post taught me something or made something click, reshare it with my own thoughts added. This is how learning communities grow.
